## Elevator pitch

> Drop in a PDF, get spaced-repetition flashcards back, and chat with the document — with the embedding model and the LLM both running on the phone via `react-native-executorch`. Nothing leaves the device.

## What it is

A React Native app with three loops:

1. **Ingest loop.** A PDF is extracted and chunked (~512 tokens, 64 overlap), each chunk embedded on-device by MiniLM-L6-v2 (384-dim) and stored in SQLite as a Float32 `BLOB`.
2. **Card-gen + chat loop.** Representative chunks are sampled with k-means over the embeddings (topic coverage, not just page order), and Llama 3.2 3B generates structured Q/A + cloze cards from them; near-duplicate questions are dropped by cosine similarity. Chat does top-k retrieval over the same vectors and answers with page-cited evidence.
3. **Review loop.** Cards are scheduled with FSRS-4.5 (a TypeScript port). The heatmap on the portfolio tile is the daily-review view.

All of it runs without a network once the models are downloaded. The phone is the server.

## The problem I was solving

Notes apps make me file things. They don't make me _learn_ things. Anki is the gold standard for the review loop but every card is hand-authored — there's no path from "I read 200 pages" to "I now have 30 well-tuned cards covering it".

The bet: combine retrieval-augmented card generation with spaced repetition, and run it entirely locally because the documents are personal and I don't want them in someone's cloud.

## Key decisions

1. **On-device only.** Once that's the spec, every other decision falls out: small quantised models, embeddings as `BLOB`s in SQLite, no auth surface, no sync.
2. **ExecuTorch (`react-native-executorch`).** Runs both the LLM and the embedder behind one native module, accelerated on the Apple Neural Engine and Android NNAPI, with a catalog of models that are licensed for commercial use. Model files are SHA-256 verified on download.
3. **FSRS-4.5, not SM-2.** FSRS is what Anki ships as its default scheduler and models retention better than SM-2; it's pure math, so it stays a tested pure function.
4. **Mock-model dev path.** By default the app runs against deterministic mock embeddings and canned LLM output, so every screen can be driven end-to-end without a 2.5 GB download.
5. **Skia for visualisations.** The review heatmap and retention curve draw on the UI thread at 60fps.

## Numbers

- **~20 tok/s** for Llama 3.2 3B on flagship phones (iPhone 15 Pro, Galaxy S24+)
- **384-dim** MiniLM vectors → 1,536 bytes per chunk
- **~100** Vitest cases, ~500ms run
- **0 bytes** of personal data leaves the device
